I am Yang Liu, a Master's student in Computer Science at St. Petersburg State University. My research focuses on computational materials science, density functional theory (DFT), materials informatics, and machine-learning-assisted materials design.

My current work involves first-principles calculations, electronic structure analysis, photocatalytic materials, two-dimensional heterostructures, dopant-induced properties, and electromagnetic wave absorption materials.

## Research Interests

- Density functional theory calculations
- Computational materials science
- Materials informatics and interpretable machine learning
- Electronic structure and optical properties
- Photocatalytic materials
- Gas sensing and surface reaction mechanisms
- Electromagnetic wave absorption materials
- Two-dimensional materials and heterostructures
