I am Yang Liu, a Master's student in Computer Science at St. Petersburg State University. My research focuses on computational materials science, density functional theory (DFT), materials informatics, and AI-assisted materials discovery.

My current work combines first-principles calculations, electronic-structure analysis, photocatalytic materials, two-dimensional heterostructures, dopant-induced properties, and interpretable machine learning for materials science.

## Research Interests

- Density functional theory calculations
- Computational materials science
- Materials informatics and interpretable machine learning
- Causal inference and causal discovery for materials informatics
- Electronic structure and optical properties
- Photocatalytic materials
- Electromagnetic wave absorption materials
- Two-dimensional materials and heterostructures

## Selected Research Topics

My previous and ongoing research includes electronic structure and optical properties of semiconductor heterojunctions, oxygen-vacancy-modified photocatalysts, dopant-induced magnetism in two-dimensional materials, microwave absorption materials, and causal discovery methods for materials informatics.

I have authored or co-authored work in journals including Industrial Chemistry & Materials, Journal of Alloys and Compounds, Physical Chemistry Chemical Physics, Molecules, Chemical Physics Letters, Carbon, Chemical Engineering Journal, and Applied Materials Today. My current manuscripts explore causal inference and interpretable machine learning for materials discovery.
