## Research Areas

My research interests lie at the intersection of computational materials science, first-principles calculations, materials informatics, and AI for Science.

### Density Functional Theory

I use DFT calculations to study electronic structures, density of states, band structures, charge transfer, optical properties, and surface reaction mechanisms.

### Photocatalytic Materials

My previous work includes semiconductor heterojunctions, oxygen-vacancy engineering, bismuth oxyhalide-based photocatalysts, and visible-light-driven pollutant degradation.

### Materials Informatics and AI for Science

My current research explores causal inference, interpretable machine learning, and causal discovery for understanding structure-property relationships in materials. I am especially interested in AI-assisted scientific reasoning workflows that connect domain knowledge, simulation data, and interpretable models.

### Electromagnetic Wave Absorption

I am also interested in microwave absorption materials, magnetic–dielectric coupling mechanisms, and interface-engineered composite systems.

### Two-Dimensional Materials and Heterostructures

I study how dopants, interfaces, defects, and external fields influence electronic, optical, magnetic, and response properties in low-dimensional materials.
